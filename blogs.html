How the acoustic analysis actually works.

Most language apps in 2026 use cloud-based speech recognition. Your audio is sent to a server, matched against an expected word sequence, and you get a pass/fail on whether you said the right words. It's word-matching dressed up as pronunciation feedback — and it's exactly what reviewers on Copycat Cafe and LanguaTalk call out as "lenient scoring that masks real errors."

Sky ENG measures your actual audio waveform, on your device, in real time. Five acoustic dimensions. Five separate scores.

The five metrics

  • Pronunciation Score — phoneme accuracy and word stress. 95% means 95 of every 100 phonemes were correctly articulated. This is the closest measure to what an IELTS examiner tracks when scoring pronunciation.
  • Clarity Score — derived from RMS (root mean square) amplitude variance across your 30-second window. Even RMS energy means consistent articulation and projection. Trailing off at sentence endings, volume dips, or over-emphasising syllables all reduce this score.
  • Pace Score — compares your WPM to the ~150 WPM optimal range for clear academic English. Too fast: clarity fractures. Too slow: hesitation is signalled. The 140–165 WPM band is where examiners give the highest delivery marks.
  • Fluency Score — a composite measuring natural flow, coherence across turns, and absence of unnatural gaps. Maps directly to the "fluency and coherence" descriptor in IELTS speaking assessment.
  • Pause Analysis — detects silent gaps above 0.5 seconds using silence segmentation. Reports total count and average duration. Two to four pauses per minute averaging 0.3–0.6 seconds is typical for fluent speech; more or longer gaps are flagged explicitly.

Conversation &
Feedback Modes.

Every session begins with a mode choice. Understanding this is how you use the app to its full potential rather than treating both modes the same.

Conversation Mode

You speak freely with the AI — no correction mid-sentence, no interruption. The AI responds naturally and the conversation continues. This mode is for building flow, confidence, and spontaneous speech. It simulates the conditions of an IELTS speaking interview: you need to produce extended, connected speech without stopping to review each sentence. The acoustic analysis runs silently and delivers your report at the end.

Who this is for

Learners who freeze up when speaking English. People preparing for interviews or visa appointments. Anyone building the habit of holding a sustained spoken exchange without second-guessing every word.

Feedback Mode

Same conversation structure, but the AI pauses after each response to deliver written analysis. After you speak, you receive:

  • Corrected sentence versions — your sentence rewritten with specific changes highlighted, so you see exactly what improved
  • Vocabulary upgrades — context-appropriate alternatives that sound more academic, more natural, or more precisely suited to the register you need
  • Structural improvements — active vs passive flags, tense consistency, complex sentence construction that pushes toward what IELTS "grammatical range" rewards
  • Register coaching — notes on formality level so you're not too casual for academic contexts or too stiff for conversational ones

Session flow

  1. Select Conversation or Feedback mode from the main menu
  2. Choose a topic or let the AI propose an exam-category prompt
  3. Tap the microphone — up to 30 seconds per turn
  4. Listen to Sky's response, then continue or review your feedback card
  5. Complete the session and receive your full five-metric acoustic report

Reading aloud practice —
word-by-word analysis.

Most people can hear their pronunciation is off on certain words. Fewer can identify which words, or why. Reading Practice solves this by comparing your spoken version of a passage against the original text word by word — not just whether you finished the sentence, but which specific words were mispronounced or missed.

How it works

  1. Choose a category — from 20+ topics: Business, Technology, Science, Literature, Travel, Health, Finance, Culture, Environment, and more
  2. Sky reads first — the 140-word passage is read aloud with natural intonation and pacing as your model
  3. Your reading — read the same passage aloud, up to 60 seconds
  4. Word-by-word comparison — your speech is mapped against the original at the word level
  5. Pronunciation report — mispronounced words, dropped words, and recurring patterns highlighted with targeted feedback

Each session generates a fresh passage — the same text never repeats. This also functions as vocabulary exposure: you encounter words in context before saying them, which is exactly how the PTE Read Aloud task is structured.

Exam alignment

PTE Read Aloud — the closest match: read a passage, scored on oral fluency and pronunciation. IELTS Speaking — fluency and pronunciation criteria trained through connected text. CELPIP — clarity and intelligibility across longer speech samples. TOEFL — delivery and intelligibility on connected prose.

Vocabulary &
translation lookup.

Two tools that are easy to overlook but consistently useful across exam prep and daily English study.

Word Definition Lookup

Type or paste any English word and receive an explanation that goes beyond a dictionary definition: contextual usage examples, formality register notes (is this word too informal for IELTS writing?), and common collocations. The IELTS "lexical resource" criterion explicitly rewards knowing how and when to use a word, not just what it means. This tool directly trains that dimension.

Translation Lookup

Type a word or phrase in your native language and get the English equivalent with pronunciation guidance, usage context, and register notes. Useful mid-session when the English word you need won't come, or when building topic-specific vocabulary before a TOEFL or CELPIP practice session.

Privacy note

Both lookup tools run on-device. No searches are logged, stored, or transmitted. Your vocabulary searches are as private as everything else in Sky ENG.

The effective exam prep approach

Alternate between modes with a purpose. Use Feedback Mode three times a week to build grammatical accuracy and lexical range. Use Conversation Mode and Roleplay twice a week to build fluency under real pressure. Use Reading Practice to directly target your specific pronunciation weaknesses. Use Listening Practice for summarization and comprehension. Track the five scores session by session — identify your single lowest metric and focus there first. Targeted single-metric improvement reaches 90%+ across the board faster than trying to improve all five simultaneously.

  • Fill the full 30 seconds every turn. Fluency metrics penalize very short responses. Extended connected speech is exactly what IELTS Part 2 and TOEFL Independent Speaking demand.
  • Vary vocabulary and sentence structures. Feedback Mode rewards lexical range. Push for less familiar synonyms; avoid repeating the same adjectives across turns.

  • Target 140–165 WPM. Most non-native speakers think speaking slower means speaking clearer. The data doesn't support this. That pace range is where examiners give the highest delivery marks.
