How the acoustic analysis works

Acoustic Speech Analysis

Measures RMS energy, peak amplitude variance, silence segmentation, and speaking-pause ratio — the same metrics used in official language testing.

Exam-Grade Scoring

90%+ across metrics typically correlates to IELTS Band 7.5–9.0, TOEFL 26–30, PTE 79–90, and CELPIP Level 10–12.

Roleplay & Conversation

Practice real-life scenarios, job interviews, academic discussions, and exam-style prompts with an AI tutor that responds to what you actually say.

Word Games

Vocabulary challenges, spelling games, reading and listening comprehension — all integrated with live AI feedback on your English.

Private by Design

All analysis happens on your device. No recordings are uploaded. No personal data is collected or stored in the cloud. Ever.
